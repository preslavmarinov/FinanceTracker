Export Report

Generate a detailed CSV report you can open in Excel or Google Sheets. Includes transactions, category breakdowns, and budget utilization.

What's included

  • Summary — Total income, expense, and net balance for the period
  • Budget Overview — Monthly limits vs actual spend per category
  • Income by Category — All income sources broken down by category
  • Expense by Category — All expenses broken down by category
  • Transactions — Every transaction with date, title, category, type, amount, and notes
